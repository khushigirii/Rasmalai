Page 15 • The birthday letter

Okay. Enough jokes.

My Rasmalai,

First of all, happy birthday to the prettiest, cutest, most stubborn girl I know. I still find it so funny that we met on the very first day of college and somehow took until second year to properly find each other. Like, hello? We were right there the whole time.

And now look at us. Making reels, laughing over the stupidest things, having conversations that make sense only to us, and becoming extremely dedicated students exactly one day before the exam. Our plan to attend lectures and study from day one really deserved better execution.

But somewhere in between all of this, you became one of my favourite people. I love how you have slowly started talking like me. I love how we can be completely unserious together, and then suddenly have the most real conversations. I love how you tolerate my endless "no" and my very unreliable college attendance. More than anything, I love that I found someone who feels like my kind of person.

You have such a lovely heart, Sara. When you love someone, you give them so much of yourself. I hope you know you deserve that same kind of love back, every single time. You deserve to be chosen, celebrated, listened to and loved without having to ask.

Also, a small announcement: boys may enter with dramatic background music, confusing texts and entirely unnecessary plot twists, but you and I are the actual long-running series. If a cute one shows up, of course we'll investigate him thoroughly. But no male lead is stealing the best-friend screen time. Sorry, not sorry.

I know this is our last year of college, and that makes me a little emotional. But I refuse to let you become just a college memory. I want to keep making reels with you, listening to your stories, laughing until our stomachs hurt and being there through all the versions of life we haven't met yet.

I genuinely wish everyone gets a Sara in their life. Someone so lovely, loyal, funny and warm. Just not my Sara, okay? She's already booked. By me. For life.

Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. Thank you for finding your way into my life, for staying, for handling me, and for being you. I love you more than I can put into words — which is saying a lot, because you know I have plenty of words.

Always your favourite headache,
Khushi ♡
